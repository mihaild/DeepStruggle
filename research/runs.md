# The arm registry — every arm, and where its result is written

One row per arm: what it varied, its budget, its directory under `data/checkpoints/`, and the
outcome with a link to the writeup. An arm with no writeup says so — an unlisted arm is how arms get
re-run. **Condensed 2026-10-15:** each row keeps the change, the control and the outcome with its
key numbers; launch notes, flag diffs, interim readings and pre-registrations are in git history
(`git show e5e8a7c:research/runs.md`). `data/reports/…` files cited below are untracked.

* Naming: [`method/run_nomenclature.md`](method/run_nomenclature.md); the A/R codes:
  [`architectures_and_recipes.md`](architectures_and_recipes.md); old names in the new grammar:
  [`run_name_map.md`](run_name_map.md). By question rather than arm: [`questions.md`](questions.md).
* **Measured twice, accepted per seat (owner, 2026-09-29):** late snapshots against the pinned panel
  *and* the SWA of the last 80M; an arm is accepted when significantly above the control's own
  self-play rate in one seat and not significantly below it in the other
  ([`method/running_experiments.md`](method/running_experiments.md)). Unless stated, every rating is
  greedy, 1,000 games a side, and "per seat +x / +y" is US / USSR over the control.
* **E7 late branches** start from the A8 heads run's 6,400M state (`E7-A8-R1-S44@6400M`, plateau
  6,800M) and are read against its own 6,400 → 6,800M leg ("the plain leg").
* Pre-E6 run directories were archived on 2026-09-29 to `data/archive/{E4_ladder,E5_ladder,E6_intermediate}/`.
  E3 and earlier (pre-P17 engine) are in [`archive/E3_ladder/`](archive/E3_ladder/README.md).
