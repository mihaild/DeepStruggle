# The seed-44 line's SWA at 4,720-4,800M against the E7-02+03+04+05 soup (2026-10-05)

Owner: run a tournament of the latest SWA (`_swa_line_ctl/E7line_swa_4720-4800M.pt`, the seed-44
line continued without the league to 4,800M) against `_soups/shallow_E7-02+03+04+05_1200M.pt`, and
generate a few games between them.

**Method.** `tools/tournament.py`, greedy (temperature 0), 1,000 games per side on paired deals.
Per the seat rule (`research/method/measurement_pitfalls.md`), the control's own greedy self-play
gives the bar in each seat: the soup against itself (a symlinked second path), 1,000 games per side.
Reports: `data/eval/tournaments/swa4720-4800_vs_soup1200.{md,json}`,
`data/eval/tournaments/soup1200_selfplay.{md,json}`.

The SWA wins 2 of these 6 (one per seat) -- six sampled games are anecdotes; the tournament above is
the measurement. A first set of six (before the labelling fix below) went 5-1 to the SWA; they were
regenerated, and `play_match.py` seeds the deal but not the network's sampling, so the same seed
does not replay the same game.

**A fix on the way.** `play_match.py --us <ckpt> --ussr <ckpt>` could not load either model: its
`NeuralBot` chose between V1 and V2 itself and rebuilt every LadderNet checkpoint as a V1 network,
which `load_state_dict` refused (self-play replays were unaffected; they load through
`NeuralAgent`). `bot/neural_bot.py` now loads through `NeuralAgent.from_checkpoint` and refuses the
checkpoints it cannot feed (merged view, feature blocks); `tests/training/test_neural_bot_checkpoint.py`.

**And a labelling fix** (owner: cards played for influence were shown as played for space). Three
hand-written copies of the pre-P17 play-mode map `{0: EVENT, 1: OPS, 2: SPACE}` had survived P17's
reordering of `ts.Resolution` (SPACE 2 -> 1; OPS split into INFLUENCE 2, COUP 3, REALIGN 4):
`play_match.format_action_description` labelled every Influence play in a match replay "Space
Race" and every space race play "Operations"; `self_play`'s blunder check scored Influence plays as
space race plays and skipped coups and realignments (the replays' own labels were right); and
`defcon_endings._mode_name` named them SPACE / PASS (its classification reads only EVENT, which is 0
either way). All three now derive from `ts.Resolution` (`blunders._play_mode`, which had already
been fixed for this); `tests/training/test_play_mode_names.py`. The match replays above were
regenerated with the correct labels.
