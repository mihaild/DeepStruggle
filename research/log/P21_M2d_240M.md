## Reading

* **240M beats 160M on both seats**, 61% as USSR and 53% as US: **+31.9 Elo** for the third 80M
  against +139.7 for the second in this field. The slope tapers, but it is still positive.
* **There is a dip at 200M**: −74.1 against 160M, below `@120M`, and then a full recovery. It is
  the same shape as E4-02-01's isolated 200M US-seat dip in
  [`E4_round_robin_240M.md`](E4_round_robin_240M.md). Here it is both seats, and deeper on US.
* **The US seat caught up.** The whole-field side gap goes from +10 pp at 120–200M to −0.1 pp at
  240M, with no sign of a side collapse.

## What this does not establish

**n = 1.** One seed, and the 200M dip shows how far a single snapshot can move. "The slope tapers
to ~+30 per 80M" needs seeds 4 and 5 taken to 240M before it is a claim; the dip's recurrence
across arms is suggestive, not measured.
