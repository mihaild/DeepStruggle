# Paper Review: KL-Regularized Search & $\pi\text{KL}$ Planning

**Title**: *Modeling Strong and Human-Like Gameplay with KL-Regularized Search*  
**Authors**: Jacob Austin, Noam Brown, Anton Bakhtin, Adam Lerer et al. (Meta AI / FAIR)  
**Publication**: *ICML* (2022) / *arXiv* 2110.05389

---

## 1. Core Problem Addressed
In imperfect-information and simultaneous-move games:
1. Standard MCTS is mathematically unsound because it assumes state independence and ignores hidden information sets.
2. Unconstrained Nash equilibrium solvers (like raw CFR) produce hyper-specialized, brittle strategies that drift far from the global policy distribution.

---

## 2. Algorithmic Innovation: $\pi\text{KL}$-Hedge
The paper introduces **$\pi\text{KL}$-Hedge**, a regret-minimization search algorithm where decision-time planning is regularized toward an anchor policy $\pi_{\text{anchor}}$:

$$\max_{\sigma_1} \min_{\sigma_2} \mathbb{E} [ Q(s, a_1, a_2) ] - \tau D_{\text{KL}}(\sigma_1 \parallel \pi_{\text{anchor}, 1}) + \tau D_{\text{KL}}(\sigma_2 \parallel \pi_{\text{anchor}, 2})$$

### The Multiplicative Weight Update Rule:
At each iteration $t$ of local subgame planning:
$$\sigma^{(t+1)}(a \mid s) \propto \pi_{\text{anchor}}(a \mid s) \cdot \exp\left( \frac{\sum_{k=1}^t Q^{(k)}(s, a)}{\tau} \right)$$

---

## 3. Theoretical Interaction with NashPG

$\pi\text{KL}$ Search and **NashPG** share a unified mathematical foundation based on **Bregman / Mirror Descent Regularization**:

| Level | Method | Objective | Purpose |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Global Training** | **NashPG** | $\max_\theta \mathbb{E}[A] - \eta D_{\text{KL}}(\pi_\theta \parallel \pi_{\text{ref}}^{(k)})$ | Guarantees monotonic convergence to global Nash equilibrium during offline self-play. |
| **Local Inference** | **$\pi\text{KL}$ Search** | $\max_\sigma \mathbb{E}[Q] - \tau D_{\text{KL}}(\sigma \parallel \pi_\theta)$ | Real-time C++ subgame search to eliminate multi-step tactical traps (like AR7 suicides). |
| **Combined System** | **Search-in-the-Loop NashPG** | Trajectories generated by $\sigma = \text{Search}(\pi_\theta, V_\theta)$ | Self-improving flywheel: the policy network continually distills the search output. |
