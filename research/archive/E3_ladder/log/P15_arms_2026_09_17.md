# The arms of 2026-09-17, and what each of them showed

One index for a day that produced a dozen scattered measurements. Every number here is taken from
the tournament JSON that produced it, under `/workspace/data/tournaments/`. Elo is **never**
comparable across blocks; the within-block gaps are the readable quantity.

Two starting points, and the distinction matters for reading step counts: arms resumed from
`p28_200M` have counters that sit **on top of** that checkpoint's own 200M, while from-scratch arms
count from zero.

## Running, and what they decide

* **`E3-31-28`** — search CE at the 5M anchor. The decisive point is **25M**, where `E3-29-28` had
  already lost 413 Elo. Behind-but-standing is a win for the anchor hypothesis; collapsing on the
  same schedule points instead at the CE coefficient, which holds ~50% of the raw gradient at 0.5.
* **`E3-30-28`** — no search at the 5M anchor, from scratch. It cannot yet answer what it was
  launched for, because **it has not given a side away**: there is nothing for a slower anchor to
  have prevented. The question is whether it eventually turns the way `E3-20-28` did, and that
  needs the full 240M with a per-seat rating at the end.
